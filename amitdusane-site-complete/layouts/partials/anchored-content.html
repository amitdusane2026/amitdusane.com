These ids are also permanent URLs once a section is published, so they are cut on a word boundary rather than mid-word, and de-duplicated if two headings ever match. The opening-tag match must tolerate attributes: 20 headings carry data-nav for the in-page spine, and a literal `

` pattern silently skipped every one of them. This lives in a partial because it now has two consumers. lesson/single.html renders it, and _default/list.json splits it to build the per-world search index. Duplicating the rule would let the two drift, and a search result pointing at an anchor the page does not have is invisible until somebody clicks it: the browser silently lands at the top instead. Same reason the monogram is one partial rather than six copies. Takes a page, returns the content with ids injected.
