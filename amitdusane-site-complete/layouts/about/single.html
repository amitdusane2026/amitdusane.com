Why this site exists

Almost all of my {{ $yrsWords }} years in digital analytics have gone into Adobe Analytics, and in recent years into the wider Experience Platform stack that has grown around it: the Web SDK and the Edge, XDM and datastreams, and Customer Journey Analytics. Most of that time I have spent explaining it, to developers, to marketers, and to stakeholders who needed a decision rather than a feature list.

What I kept running into is that the documentation answers how, and the question in the room is nearly always why. Why does this feature exist, what problem was it built for, and what does it cost you to get the decision wrong. Those answers were in my head and in my notes, and nowhere a stranger could reach them. That is what these guides are: the reasoning, written down, free to read, and meant to sit beside the official documentation rather than in front of it.

I also write them to think properly. Explaining something to somebody who does not already know it is the fastest way to find the parts you only half understood.
