--- title: "Data Feeds" seotitle: "Data Feeds in Adobe Analytics" tagline: "Raw hit-level data" description: "Data feeds hand you raw hit-level data, one row per hit. What is in the columns, what is missing, and what you have to rebuild yourself." type: module modulenum: 17 weight: 17 ---

Every tracking call your site sends becomes one row in a text file. Data feeds are how you get those rows, and they are the layer everything else in Adobe Analytics is built on top of.

This module covers what a feed is, how to configure one, and what actually arrives: a file with no header row, lookup tables full of numeric codes, and columns that only make sense once you know how Adobe stores them. The last section is the honest one. Counting raw rows is easy, and getting the same answer Adobe gets is not.
