For most people who use Adobe Analytics, Analysis Workspace is the product. It is the blank canvas where questions actually get asked.

This module covers how it is put together, from projects and panels down to tables and visualizations, and then the analyses that answer harder questions: cohorts, fallout and flow, and anomaly detection. It also covers the parts nobody teaches. Curating a project so somebody else can read it, sharing one without accidentally handing over ownership, and why the project everybody depends on is usually the slowest in the company.
