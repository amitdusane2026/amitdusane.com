Almost every real question narrows to which people. Downloads in North America. Visitors who abandoned a cart. Customers who bought twice. A segment is how you say that once and then use it everywhere.

This module covers what a segment actually is, and the choice that governs everything else about it: the container, which decides whether you are asking about a single hit, one visit, or a person's entire history. It covers the operators and sequences that let you ask about the order things happened in, how segments are shared, and the habits that keep a segment library usable instead of a graveyard of near duplicates.
