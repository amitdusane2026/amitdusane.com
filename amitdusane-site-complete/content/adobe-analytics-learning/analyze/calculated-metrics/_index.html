A count can point at the wrong winner. The page with the most visits is often not the page doing the most work, and the honest answer usually arrives as a ratio rather than a total.

Calculated metrics are how you build those ratios. This module covers what they really are, and how settings inside the builder change the answer in ways that are easy to miss, so that what looks like one formula is usually at least four. It ends with the governance problem: anybody can build one in a minute, and two years later the library holds four different Conversion Rates and nobody knows which is right.
