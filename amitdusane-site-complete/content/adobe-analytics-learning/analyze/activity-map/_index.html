Page reports tell you people arrived. They cannot tell you which of the four buttons on that page anybody pressed.

Activity Map answers that. This module covers how Adobe works out a link's name, what to do when that name turns out to be useless, and the two quite different ways of reading the data: an overlay on the live page, and dimensions inside Workspace. It ends with where this data misleads, because click reports flatter pages that are simply busy, and single page applications complicate them further.
