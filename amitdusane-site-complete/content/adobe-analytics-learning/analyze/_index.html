Collected and shaped data still answers nothing until somebody asks it a question. This is the phase where that happens.

The work here is less about the interface than about the question. Which people are you actually asking about. What number would settle the argument in the room. Which touchpoint deserves the credit for a sale. The tools themselves are learnable in a week. Deciding what to ask, and recognizing when an answer should not be trusted, is the part that takes longer and matters more.
