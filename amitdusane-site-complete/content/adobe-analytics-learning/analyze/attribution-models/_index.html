Three teams report three different order counts for the same month, and all three are right. That is an attribution disagreement, and it is one of the most common arguments in analytics.

This module explains what attribution is really doing, compares the models Adobe offers and shows the three shapes underneath them, and covers lookback windows, which decide which touchpoints exist at all. It ends with choosing one. There is no correct model, but there is a correct way to arrive at a choice and defend it in a room full of people who wanted a different answer.
