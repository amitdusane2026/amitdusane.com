VISTA rules sit further back in the pipeline than anything else you can reach, and Adobe configures them rather than you. They can do things no other tool can.

This module starts from no. Most problems that look like VISTA problems are cheaper to solve somewhere else, so it works through those alternatives first, then shows the jobs that genuinely need one: copying hits into a second report suite, routing traffic, and a handful of cases with no other answer. A short module, and mostly about knowing when not to reach for it.
