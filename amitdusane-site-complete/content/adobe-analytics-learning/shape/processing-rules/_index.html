Processing rules run the moment data arrives, before any of it is stored. They are how you correct a value, fill a gap, or standardize something inconsistent without waiting for a developer release.

This module covers what they can and cannot do, how conditions and actions fit together, and five worked examples built one at a time. The last section is the one that matters. Processing rules are immediate, permanent and not retroactive: a rule written today does nothing for yesterday's data, and a rule that overwrites a value destroys the original.
