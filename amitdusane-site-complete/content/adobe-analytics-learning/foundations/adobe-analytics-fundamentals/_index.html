Most people meet Adobe Analytics as a screen full of reports and never ask why it exists at all, when the business already has server logs, a CRM and an order system.

This module answers that first, because the answer shapes everything after it. It covers what the tool is actually for, the handful of terms you need before anything else makes sense, how it differs from Google Analytics in the ways that matter, and who controls access to it. A short module, and the one that stops the rest feeling arbitrary.
