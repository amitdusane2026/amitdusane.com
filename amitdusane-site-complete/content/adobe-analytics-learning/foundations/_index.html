Every implementation decision made later rests on three things: knowing what Adobe Analytics actually is, knowing where its data lands, and knowing which variables carry that data. Skip them and the rest becomes guesswork that happens to work, right up until the day it does not.

This category covers those three. It explains why the tool exists when server logs and a CRM are already in the business, how report suites decide what gets counted together, and what props, eVars, and events are really for. Read it in order. The categories that follow assume it.
