A report suite is where your data lands. Deciding how many you need is the first architectural choice of any implementation, and it is one you live with for years.

This module covers the choice between one global suite and several, virtual report suites for slicing what you already collect, and the settings that quietly govern what gets counted at all. It also covers bot filtering, which removes automated traffic before anyone sees it, and data retention, because how long you keep personal data is decided here rather than later.
