Cross-Device Analytics (CDA) stitches visitor data across devices to provide a unified view of the customer journey, reducing duplicate visitor counts and improving attribution.

Stitching Methods

MethodHow It WorksRequirements
Field-BasedUses a consistent identifier (login ID) you provideAuthenticated users, Customer ID set
Device GraphUses Adobe's Co-op or Private GraphDevice Graph subscription

How CDA Works

Cross-Device Stitching Example
Before CDA:
├── Mobile Visitor A: Browse products
├── Desktop Visitor B: Add to cart
├── Tablet Visitor C: Complete purchase
└── Result: 3 unique visitors, unclear journey

After CDA (same person logged in):
├── Person 1 on Mobile: Browse products
├── Person 1 on Desktop: Add to cart
├── Person 1 on Tablet: Complete purchase
└── Result: 1 unique visitor, complete journey visible

Benefits:
- Accurate unique visitor counts
- Complete conversion paths
- Better attribution across touchpoints
- Reduced audience duplication
ℹ️ Virtual Report Suite Required

CDA works through Virtual Report Suites with cross-device stitching enabled. The base report suite contains raw data; the VRS provides the stitched view.
