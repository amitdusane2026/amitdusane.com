Every number in every report began as a tracking call leaving a browser. This module is about those calls and what they carry.

It covers the two kinds of call and why there are two at all, what each one costs in billing terms, and the identity problem sitting underneath everything: how Adobe decides two visits came from the same person, why that breaks the moment somebody crosses a domain, and why cookies keep disappearing. Single page applications get their own section, because they break tracking in two distinct ways that look identical from outside.
