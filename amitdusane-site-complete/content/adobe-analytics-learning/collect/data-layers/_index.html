A data layer is an agreement between the people who build a website and the people who measure it. The site publishes its values in a known shape, and analytics reads them without needing to understand how the page works.

This module covers what that agreement looks like in practice, the Adobe Client Data Layer and whether you actually need it, and how to design one that survives a site rewrite. The design part matters most. A data layer built to mirror how the site happens to work today becomes a liability the moment the site changes.
