A rejection is the friendliest failure in this whole system, because unlike almost everything else on mobile, it tells you what is wrong.

XDM is checked. When a payload does not match the schema, the Edge refuses it and returns an error response, which appears in Assurance in place of the post-processed data you were expecting.

The message names the field and the problem: a path that does not exist, a value of the wrong type, a required field missing.

Three causes account for most of them.

RenamedA field was renamed in the schema after the app was built, so the app is sending a path that no longer exists.
RetypedA value that used to be text is arriving where a number is expected.
SpellingThe app spells the path differently from the schema, usually a capital letter.

The first two are fixed on your side. Even the third begins with you supplying the exact path rather than describing it.

For your implementation: read the error before forming a theory. It is genuinely quicker than reasoning about what might have changed, and the field it names is almost always the field at fault.

⚑ Architect’s Decision

Never rename or retype a field in a live schema on the assumption that apps will catch up. Every already-installed version keeps sending the old shape, and each of those events is rejected until its user updates.
