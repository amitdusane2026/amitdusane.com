The extension catalogue is long, most of it belongs to products you are not implementing, and installing extras is not free.

Four cover an Edge Network implementation.

Edge NetworkSends the events, and holds the datastream for each environment.
Edge IdentityManages the ECID and any identifiers you add. Edge will not work without it, so the two are one decision rather than two.
Edge ConsentCarries the collect preference and the default that applies before anybody has answered.
AssuranceHow you see inside a running app, which on mobile is not optional.

Mobile Core is always present and is not something you install. Lifecycle comes along with the modern startup call.

Everything else in the catalogue belongs to a product: Messaging, Optimize, Places, Target. Each one adds size to the app and settings to maintain, and a mobile developer will reasonably ask why an analytics project is making their app bigger.

For your implementation: ask for four, name them exactly, and resist adding a fifth in case it is useful. Extensions are added later without difficulty, and every one you carry unused is weight somebody else is paying for.

⚑ Architect’s Decision

Four extensions, and Assurance is one of them from day one rather than added when something breaks. Adding it during an incident means waiting for a release at the worst possible moment.
