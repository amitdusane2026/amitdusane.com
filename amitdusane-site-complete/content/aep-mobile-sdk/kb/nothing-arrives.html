An empty report is not a diagnosis. It is the same symptom for at least five different problems, and guessing between them is what costs the weeks.

The first split is the useful one. Did the event leave the phone at all, or did it leave and get refused?

Those two have nothing in common except how they look in a report, and they are owned by different people.

Never sentNothing in Assurance beyond the client column, or nothing at all. The SDK did not start, a consent value is blocking collection, or a phone with no connection is holding events in the queue.
Sent, refusedThe event appears, travels, and produces an error response instead of post-processed data. The payload reached Adobe and did not match the schema.

The first case is usually a wrong Environment File ID, or a setting you own, or something that resolves itself when the phone finds a signal. The second is a schema or field-path problem rather than an app problem.

For your implementation: decide which of the two you have before telling anybody anything. Saying that events are not being sent, when they are being sent and rejected, points a whole team at the wrong system for as long as it takes them to prove you wrong.

⚑ Architect’s Decision

Open Assurance before opening a ticket, every single time. Five minutes there decides whether this is a conversation with one person or a sprint for a team.
