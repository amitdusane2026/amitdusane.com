Three faults in the current mark, before you choose

Two reds. The bar is #C8102E, the site accent is #e11d48. Close but not equal reads as a mistake, not a decision.
A third brand colour. The ink is navy #1E3A8A, alongside crimson and the migration guide's blue.
The bar dies small. 3.5 units in a 64 viewBox is about 1.4px at byline size. It becomes a smudge.

My recommendation

B for the byline and the header, C for the favicon.

B keeps the mark you already have and only removes what is inconsistent about it: the second red and the third brand colour. It stays light enough to sit beside your name without shouting, which matters when it is about to appear on 116 pages. The thicker bar survives 26px.

C is genuinely the strongest small, and that is exactly what a favicon needs — a 16px tab icon has to be a shape, not a letterform. Using the solid badge there and the open mark everywhere else is normal practice, not an inconsistency.

Whichever you pick, it goes into layouts/partials/logo.html first. The mark currently lives in six copies, so every future change is six edits and one of them will get missed. That is IDN-04 and it should be done as part of this, not after.
