<p>Raw collected data is rarely the data anyone wants to read. Values arrive inconsistent, campaign traffic arrives uncredited, and codes arrive where names belong. This category covers the layer that sits between collection and reporting.</p>

<p>Sequence is the whole point here. Processing rules run at collection time and cannot be undone. VISTA rules run before them and are rarely the right answer. Marketing channels decide who gets credit. Classifications add meaning after the fact, without touching what was collected. Knowing which lever to reach for, and when it fires, is what separates a fixable implementation from a permanent mistake.</p>
