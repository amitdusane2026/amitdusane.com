How to use this guide

Not a click-by-click manual. A guide to why Adobe Analytics works the way it does, how the pieces fit, and the decisions that separate an implementation that lasts from one that quietly breaks.

Every module starts with a real problem, explains why the technology exists before how it works, then connects the business need to the architecture, the implementation, and the practical experience. The aim is to leave you thinking like an implementation architect, not just knowing which menu to open.

It is intentionally not a product manual. Adobe Experience League already documents where to click and how to configure each screen.

Use this guide
to understand what to build, why it matters, and how to think about it.
Use Adobe documentation
to learn where to click and how to configure it.

Together they cover the whole picture.
