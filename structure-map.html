The decisions this map is built on

All closed. Nothing about the structure is open.

1. Google Analytics

Title drops the 4. It ages better and matches what people type.

2. SDR is a module

Six sections, and it now sits before Testing and Debugging. Design, build, test. Validation and Sign-off closes the SDR and gives testing something to prove.

2b. Category renamed

Govern and maintain becomes Deliver and maintain, and the folder becomes /deliver/. The category now holds testing, the design document, and the handover to CJA.

3. Tag Management Systems

Named for the hook. The section owns the general idea, the Launch module owns the product.

4. ECID gets a section

Placed before Cross-Domain Tracking, so the ID exists before anything explains how it survives.

5. Calculated Metrics

Replaces Dimensions and Metrics. Three sections. Small topic, high impact.

6. Attribution Models

New module of four. Nav label stays the short word Attribution.

7. and, not &

Applied to every category, module and section title. Zero address risk.

8. APIs

No module, no section. One surface mention inside Data Feeds, which is already the getting-data-out module.

9. Governance module dropped

Six stubs of theory replaced by one module built on a real artifact.

Titles are lean now

Section names are short and shaped like what people type. The longer explanatory phrasings become H3 headings inside the section, which is where they belonged.

10. Privacy, closed

Two sections added to Report Suites: Bot Filtering, and Privacy and Data Retention. Both are report suite settings, so that is where they belong. No privacy module. Consent stays in the Launch module.

11. Workspace, closed

Ten sections confirmed, including Cohort Analysis, Fallout and Flow Analysis, Curation and Templates, and Workspace Performance and Limits.
